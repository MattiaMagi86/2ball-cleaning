01 / Services

The right clean
for the space
you're in.

Five ways to get your space back to its best — everyday upkeep, a deeper reset, a move, carpets and upholstery, or a full floor refinish. Tell us what you need; we'll quote the job.

01

Standard Cleaning

A steady, reliable clean for the rooms you use every day. We dust, tidy, disinfect and reset the space — the kind of upkeep that keeps a home or workplace feeling cared for, without you lifting a finger.

What's included

  • Dusting throughout the space
  • General tidying and organising
  • Bathrooms and toilets cleaned and disinfected
  • Bins emptied and relined
  • Kitchen surfaces and countertops sanitised
  • General surface cleaning and upkeep
02

Deep Cleaning

For spaces that need more than a once-over. Built-up dirt, grease and grime don't stand a chance — including in the spots a regular clean tends to miss.

What's included

  • Bathrooms and toilets thoroughly cleaned and disinfected
  • Detailed floor washing and cleaning
  • Walls and window sills
  • Cupboards and accessible surfaces
  • Professional carpet steaming and sanitising
  • Couch and upholstery steam cleaning
  • Kitchen appliances deep cleaned
  • Hard-to-reach and neglected areas
03

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

A fresh start, without the scrub. We clean the property from top to bottom — so whether you're handing back the keys or picking them up, it's ready for whoever comes next.

What's included

  • Bathrooms and toilets cleaned and disinfected
  • Walls and floors washed
  • Detailed kitchen cleaning
  • Appliances cleaned and degreased
  • Interior cupboards cleaned
  • Countertops, cabinets and accessible surfaces
  • All rooms and common areas
04

Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning

Bring carpets and upholstered furniture back to life. Professional steam cleaning lifts the dirt, grime and odours that a vacuum alone leaves behind.

What's included

  • Professional carpet steam cleaning
  • Deep extraction of accumulated dirt and grime
  • Treatment of heavily soiled and stained areas
  • Professional cleaning solutions applied
  • Odour neutraliser treatment
  • Upholstered furniture steam cleaned and refreshed
05

Floor Stripping & Waxing

Bring tired floors back to a smooth, polished finish. We strip the old wax and buildup, then seal the floor with a fresh protective coat.

What's included

  • Existing wax and floor buildup stripped
  • Machine scrubbing and floor preparation
  • Old finish and residue removed
  • Fresh floor finish and protective wax applied
  • Professional buffing and finishing
